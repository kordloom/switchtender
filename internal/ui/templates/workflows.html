Yardmaster

Workflow

Wire steps into a graph, then run it. A linked step starts once every step feeding into it succeeds. Unlinked steps run one at a time in the order they were added.

Step

Add a step to begin. Drag steps to arrange them, and drag from a step's right edge onto another step to make it wait. Click a link to select it, then press Delete to remove it. Ctrl+Z undoes.
