Questions

What exactly is the license file?

A small signed JSON document. The binary carries our public key and verifies the signature offline; nothing is fetched, ever. Install it with switchtender license install, see where you stand with switchtender license status. No license server, no activation, no phone-home, and losing internet changes nothing.

What happens when a license lapses?

Paid features stop; nothing else changes. Your data, your evidence, your receipts, and every Community feature keep working, the running server drops to Community without a restart, and a database you initialized on PostgreSQL stays fully usable forever. We built the gate so that it cannot hold your data hostage even if we wanted it to.

I started on SQLite. How do I get to Postgres and HA?

Team includes Postgres. Today the supported move is switchtender backup and restore, which carries your configuration: projects, templates, inventories, credential shells, schedules, and tokens. Run history and the audit chain stay with the SQLite file, and that is not a loss: the old database remains readable and its evidence verifiable forever, because receipts never depend on where the data lives now. If you want the history physically merged, tell us and we will do the move with you.

Do you count my hosts?

No. Bands are self-reported at purchase, the binary counts nothing and reports nothing, and we will never ask to audit. If your fleet grows past your band, we trust you to move up at renewal, and that trust is priced in.

Why is the change register paid when receipts are free?

Because proofs and packaging are different things. Anyone must be able to verify what your install did, on any tier, forever, or the evidence story is worthless, so the chain, anchors, receipts, dossiers, and verifier are all Community. The period change register is the assembled compliance deliverable an auditor consumes, and assembling deliverables is a thing organizations pay for.

How do I buy or trial today?

Email hello@kordloom.com with your org name and band. A signed 30-day trial license usually lands within hours, no card. Buying works the same way with an invoice until card checkout ships. Enterprise starts with a conversation.

What happens to paid features if you disappear?

Your license keeps verifying, because verification is offline, so everything works through the end of your term, and nothing you produced is ever lost: Community, your data, and your receipts work forever, and the open verifier does not depend on us. Each release also converts to Apache 2.0 two years after it ships, so the code itself outlives the company.

What does the license allow?

SwitchTender is source-available under BSL 1.1. You can read the source, modify it, self-host it, and run it in production for your own organization. The restriction is offering it as a competing hosted service. Each release converts to Apache 2.0 two years after it ships. See the LICENSE for the exact terms.

Can I move from AWX?

Yes, on Community, in one command, and the same goes for Semaphore, Rundeck, Jenkins freestyle jobs, and plain crontabs. AAP, Tower, and Ascender share the AWX export format. For a large or regulated move, the Migration Program does it for you. Start with the switching guide.
