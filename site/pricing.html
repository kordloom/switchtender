SwitchTender Migration Program

$25,000fixed price, four milestones

A founder-led program that moves you off AWX, Semaphore, or Rundeck and into production on SwitchTender, with the change controls you already run and your evidence workflows carried forward. Milestones are scheduled around your access, approvals, credential owners, and production change windows, and a delay in any of those moves the affected milestone. The four milestones are normally completed over about six weeks once the readiness items below are in place.

Before milestone one begins

  • Access provisioned to the environment, the repositories, and the target hosts.
  • One named technical counterpart who can coordinate access and answer migration questions within a business day.
  • The people who hold your credential values, since exports never contain secrets.
  • A representative set of production workflows chosen and recorded for validation, so we agree up front what "working" means.
  • Someone who can decide approval policy, and someone who can authorize the production cutover. Often two different people.

The four milestones

  1. Discovery and import. We inventory what you have, run the import, and show you item by item what was in the old controller and what landed.
  2. Environment. SwitchTender deployed on your infrastructure, sized to your fleet, wired to your identity provider, with credentials entered by their owners and exercised against the recorded validation set.
  3. Governance. Approval policy built from the change process you already run, agent policy for anything proposing changes automatically, and evidence configured against the controls you identify with us during discovery.
  4. Cutover and handoff. The validation set recorded in milestone one runs green, meaning every workflow in it completes with a zero exit status, which is what finishes this milestone rather than an open-ended parallel period. You authorize the cutover through your own change process, and you get runbooks and a working session, because your team owns this when we leave.

At handoff you have the migrated configuration running in your environment, documented identity and governance configuration, the recorded validation set passing, evidence configured against your controls, production runbooks, and a team that has been walked through all of it.

Flat $25,000 for a standard migration: one source controller, one production environment, standard integrations. Playbook and host counts do not set the price by themselves, because the importer handles bulk. We scope together first when the work changes in nature rather than size, such as multiple independently operated environments, custom execution dependencies or plugins, nonstandard network paths, air-gapped targets, or complex identity and secret-store integration. We will tell you which of those you have during the first conversation, not after you have paid.

Migration reproduces the agreed behavior of your source controller for the recorded validation set. Anything that also fails in the source environment is outside migration scope, as is new automation, architectural redesign, and rewriting existing content. We flag whatever we find early and quote it separately if you want it fixed. Work runs under a mutual NDA and a fixed-price statement of work with the milestones above. Founder-led and limited to one active migration at a time. A portion credits toward your first year of Assurance if you want us to keep the evidence running afterward.

Questions

Is it really free, or free-until-you-need-it?

Really free, and complete. No seat cap, no host cap, no execution cap, no expiry, and that includes SSO, SCIM, RBAC, the whole policy engine, Postgres with HA, and the audit chain. Nothing is unlocked with a license key, because there are no license keys. What we charge for today is work we do around the software: migrating you onto it, coordinating approvers outside it, and staying accountable for your evidence. Whatever we add later, nothing that is free today ever becomes paid.

What is actually built today?

Everything in the Free column ships in the current release, today. The Migration Program is available now and you can start one this week. Governance and Assurance are services we are building with our first design partners, which is why they say talk to us instead of checkout. You are not billed until the capability you signed up for is running, and early partners get a direct say in what we build first.

What happens at renewal?

The price we agree is fixed for your term, and we tell you before it changes at a renewal. You can cancel at the end of a term. For the design-partner services, billing starts when the first capability you signed for is actually running, not at signature. Cancelling stops the service; it never touches your install, your data, or your evidence, all of which keep working without us.

What happens to paid features if you disappear?

Nothing stops. Every feature is in the one self-hosted binary, with no license server, no online activation, and no phone-home, so the platform never needs us to run. Your chain and receipts stay verifiable offline with the open verifier, which is a separate Apache-2.0 library that does not depend on us either. Each release carries its own source tarball inside its signed manifest, the source is BSL 1.1, and every release converts to Apache 2.0 two years after it ships. What you would lose is the work we do: approver coordination, maintained mappings, custody, and support.

Why flat per org instead of per seat?

A controller gets more valuable the more people route work through it, so charging per seat punishes exactly the adoption we want, and per-node metering is the thing people are leaving. One org, one flat price, however many operators, however many runs. The only input is the size of the fleet we are covering.

What does the license allow?

SwitchTender is source-available under BSL 1.1. You can read the source, modify it, self-host it, and run it in production for your own organization, free, forever. The only restriction is offering it as a competing hosted service. Each release converts to Apache 2.0 two years after it ships. See the LICENSE for the exact terms.

Can I move from AWX?

Yes. Your AWX export imports in one command on the free tier, and the Semaphore importer is verified against a real backup from a current release. For a large or regulated move, the Migration Program does it for you. Start with the switching guide.

How do I actually buy Governance or Assurance?

Email hello@kordloom.com. There is no checkout because these are services we operate, and because we are building them with the first few organizations rather than shipping a menu. We will talk through the control problem you are actually trying to solve first. If you want something you can buy outright today, that is the Migration Program.
