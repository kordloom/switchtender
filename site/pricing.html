What stays free, always

Two promises the pricing does not touch, because charging for them would make the product dishonest.

The chain is free because it is the point

Generating the tamper-evident hash chain and verifying it locally is free forever. Gating it would make "provable" a sales gimmick. You pay for evidence packs and independent verification, not for the chain existing.

The single-operator product is complete

The controller you run today stays complete: orchestration, the live matrix, all seven tools, the chain, offline verify. The paid tiers add governance for many people and evidence for outside auditors. They do not hold the good parts hostage.

Questions

Is it really free, or free-until-you-need-it?

Free to self-host with no seat cap and no expiry. The paid tiers add capabilities the free tier does not have, such as SSO, Vault, and scheduled evidence packs. They do not remove anything from Free or meter what it already does.

Why flat per org instead of per seat?

A controller gets more valuable the more people route work through it. Charging per seat punishes exactly the adoption you want. One org, one flat price, however many operators.

What does the license allow?

SwitchTender is source-available under BSL 1.1. You can read the source, self-host it, and run it in production. The license restricts offering it as a competing hosted service. See the LICENSE for the exact terms.

Can I move from AWX?

Yes. Your AWX export imports in one command on the free tier. For a large or regulated migration, Governed includes hands-on assistance, priced separately. Start with the switching guide.
