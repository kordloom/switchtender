What stays free, always

Charging for any of these would make the product dishonest, so we never will.

What ships free stays free

Nothing ever moves from free to paid. The paid tiers are built from new capabilities, never carved out of the binary you already run. If we shipped it free, it's yours.

The chain is free because it's the point

Generating the tamper-evident hash chain and verifying it locally is free forever. Gating it would make "provable" a sales gimmick. You pay for evidence packs an auditor accepts, not for the chain existing.

The whole controller is in the free tier

Orchestration, the live matrix, all seven tools, SSO, RBAC, approvals, Postgres, the chain, offline verify. The paid tiers add governance for many people and evidence for outside auditors. They don't hold the good parts hostage.

Questions

Is it really free, or free-until-you-need-it?

Free to self-host with no seat cap, no host cap, and no expiry, and that includes SSO, RBAC, approvals, and Postgres. The paid tiers add capabilities that have never shipped, such as SCIM provisioning, external approvers, and scheduled evidence packs. Nothing moves from free to paid, ever.

What is actually built today?

Everything in the Free column ships in the current release. Team and Governed are in development with our first customers, which is why those tiers say early access instead of checkout. If you sign on early you pay as the features you need arrive, and you get a direct say in what we build first.

Why flat per org instead of per seat?

A controller gets more valuable the more people route work through it. Charging per seat punishes exactly the adoption you want. One org, one flat price, however many operators.

What does the license allow?

SwitchTender is source-available under BSL 1.1. You can read the source, modify it, self-host it, and run it in production for your own organization, free, forever. The only restriction is offering it as a competing hosted service. Each release converts to Apache 2.0 after four years. See the LICENSE for the exact terms.

Can I move from AWX?

Yes. Your AWX export imports in one command on the free tier. For a large or regulated migration, Governed includes hands-on assistance, priced separately. Start with the switching guide.
