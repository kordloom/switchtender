Why I built a one-binary alternative to AWX

AWX needs Kubernetes, Postgres, Redis, and Receptor before it runs a playbook. SwitchTender runs the same playbooks from one Go binary, paints every run as a live host-by-task matrix, and imports your AWX projects in one command. July 2026.
